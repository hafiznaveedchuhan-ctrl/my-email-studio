# Kettlebird Tea — Brand Voice

## Who we are (personality)
**Warm, friendly, a little playful.** Like an old friend calling out, "Come in, the kettle's on."
The kettle whistle is our little bird song: cheerful, never childish.

- Warm, not gushing
- Playful, not silly
- Knows tea, but never shows off
- Calm. We are the 10-minute break in someone's busy day.

## Who we talk to
Tea lovers aged **25–45** who are bored of the same teabag every day.
They want to try something new each month, and for them tea is a small
"me time" ritual, not just a drink.

They are busy, curious and a bit tired. Talk to **one** person, not a crowd.

## How we sound
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "you" and "we", short sentences | "customers", "users", corporate "Dear valued..." |
| Sensory words: steep, brew, aroma, cosy, slow sip, whistle | Hype words: revolutionary, game-changer, unbelievable, insane |
| One gentle joke or tea pun per email, at most | Pun in every line |
| Specific details (origin, tasting notes, steep time) | Vague claims ("the best tea ever") |
| Calm invitations ("Pop the kettle on") | Pressure ("ACT NOW", "LAST CHANCE!!!") |

### Words we like
steep, brew, sip, kettle, cosy, slow, whistle, small-batch, ritual, "pop the kettle on"

### Words we avoid
detox, cure, boost immunity, superfood, miracle, hurry, don't miss out, guaranteed

## Formatting
- Subject lines: sentence case, **under 45 characters**, max **one** emoji (🫖 or 🍃), never ALL CAPS.
- Preview text: 40–90 characters, adds to the subject and doesn't repeat it.
- Body: short paragraphs (1–3 lines), max ~150 words for the main message.
- **One** clear CTA per email, as a button-style line: `[Join The Steep Club →]`.
- No exclamation-mark storms. One "!" per email at most.

## Sender & sign-off
- From name: **Mina at Kettlebird** (a made-up founder persona)
- Sign-off: "Warmly, Mina & the Kettlebird flock 🐦"
- Every email ends with: "You're getting this because you said hi to Kettlebird. Unsubscribe any time."

## Never say (hard rules)
- **No health claims.** Tea does not cure, detox, heal or boost anything.
- **No fake urgency** or fake scarcity (no countdowns that aren't real, no "only 3 left!" unless true).
- **No misleading subjects** ("Re:", "Fwd:", "Your order" when there is no order).
- No making fun of other brands or of coffee drinkers. We're friendly to everyone.
- No made-up reviews or testimonials.

## Quick test before shipping
1. Would a friend actually say this out loud?
2. Is there exactly one CTA?
3. Any banned word or health claim? → fix it.
4. Subject under 45 characters, no ALL CAPS?
